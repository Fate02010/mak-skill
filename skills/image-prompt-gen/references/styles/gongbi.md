# gongbi — 工笔画

中国工笔重彩画，线条精细，色彩雅致，富贵气质。

## 核心关键词

```
Chinese Gongbi painting, meticulous brushwork, fine line drawing, mineral pigments, classical Chinese art
```

## 风格特征

- `fine hairline brushstrokes` — 精细发丝级线描
- `layered mineral colors` — 矿物质颜料层叠
- `refined decorative patterns` — 精细装饰纹样
- `Tang dynasty figure painting style` — 唐代仕女画风
- `silk or paper with aged texture` — 绢本/纸本质感
- `gold accents` — 金箔点缀

## 适合主题

花鸟工笔、仕女人物、荷花鲤鱼、宫廷场景

## 完整提示词示例

```
two mandarin ducks swimming among pink lotus flowers, traditional Chinese Gongbi painting, meticulous fine line brushwork, layered mineral pigments in soft pinks and greens, silk texture, aged paper background, gold accents on feathers, classical and elegant, highly detailed botanical elements
```
