# product-photo — 产品摄影

专业产品摄影，突出细节质感，适合电商和品牌推广。

## 核心关键词

```
product photography, clean background, professional product shot, studio lighting, high detail
```

## 背景风格

| 风格 | 关键词 |
|------|--------|
| 纯白背景 | `pure white background, clean studio` |
| 黑色高端 | `matte black background, luxury product` |
| 自然材质 | `marble surface, wooden table, lifestyle context` |
| 悬浮感 | `floating product, dramatic shadows below` |
| 飞溅特效 | `water splash, liquid burst, dynamic` |

## 光照建议

- `three-point studio lighting` — 标准产品光
- `rim lighting, product highlight` — 边缘高光强调质感
- `dramatic side lighting, long shadows` — 戏剧感

## 完整提示词示例

```
a premium glass perfume bottle with gold accents, placed on a marble surface, dramatic side lighting creating long elegant shadows, rim lighting highlighting the glass texture, professional product photography, pure white background, highly detailed reflections, 8K, commercial photography
```

## 适合主题

电商产品图、护肤品、香水、电子产品、食品饮料、手工艺品
