# watercolor — 水彩插画

柔和流动的水彩风格，温柔梦幻，纸张纹理丰富。

## 核心关键词

```
watercolor illustration, soft flowing colors, paper texture, delicate brushstrokes, translucent washes
```

## 风格变体

| 风格 | 关键词 |
|------|--------|
| 植物花卉 | `botanical watercolor, detailed flora, naturalist illustration` |
| 梦幻故事 | `dreamy watercolor, whimsical, fairy tale atmosphere` |
| 城市写生 | `urban sketching, architectural watercolor, loose brushwork` |
| 人物肖像 | `watercolor portrait, loose brushstrokes, color bleeding edges` |

## 技术特征

- `color bleeding at edges` — 颜色边缘晕染
- `wet on wet technique` — 湿润叠色效果
- `unpainted white paper showing through` — 留白效果
- `rough paper texture` — 粗粒纸张质感
- `limited palette, harmonious colors` — 色彩克制协调

## 完整提示词示例

```
a cherry blossom branch with delicate pink flowers, watercolor illustration, soft flowing colors, color bleeding at edges, delicate translucent brushstrokes, warm pastel palette, white paper showing through, botanical art style, detailed flora, gentle and dreamy atmosphere
```
