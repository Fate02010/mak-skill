# street-photo — 街拍/纪实摄影

真实的城市记录，充满人文气息，可黑白可彩色。

## 核心关键词

```
street photography, documentary style, candid, urban, authentic moment
```

## 风格变体

| 风格 | 关键词 |
|------|--------|
| 经典黑白 | `black and white street photography, high contrast, Henri Cartier-Bresson style` |
| 胶片质感 | `film grain, Fujifilm Velvia, color film, analog photography` |
| 夜间街拍 | `nighttime street photography, neon lights, wet streets, reflections` |
| 日系胶片 | `Japanese street photography, Daido Moriyama style, high contrast black and white` |

## 构图原则

- `decisive moment` — 决定性瞬间
- `leading lines, urban geometry` — 城市几何构图
- `layered composition, foreground and background` — 前景后景层次

## 完整提示词示例

```
Tokyo street at night, neon signs reflected on rain-soaked pavement, a lone figure in a yellow raincoat walking under an umbrella, street photography, documentary style, film grain, Fujifilm Velvia color film, candid moment, 35mm lens, high contrast, atmospheric
```

## 适合主题

城市人文、旅行记录、市场集市、节日庆典、老城区
