# cyberpunk — 赛博朋克

霓虹都市、黑暗未来、高科技低生活，视觉冲击力极强。

## 核心关键词

```
cyberpunk, neon lights, futuristic city, rain-soaked streets, high-tech dystopia
```

## 配色系统

- 主色：深黑/深蓝 + 霓虹粉/电子蓝/毒性绿
- `neon pink and cyan on black`
- `purple and electric blue neon glow`
- `toxic green haze`

## 技术特征

- `neon reflections on wet pavement` — 湿地面霓虹反射
- `glitch effect, digital artifacts` — 故障效果
- `holographic displays` — 全息投影
- `dense urban architecture, towering skyscrapers` — 密集摩天楼
- `fog and rain atmosphere` — 雾雨氛围
- `Blade Runner aesthetic` — 银翼杀手美学参考

## 完整提示词示例

```
a lone figure in a black trench coat walking through a neon-lit cyberpunk alley, rain-soaked streets with neon reflections, holographic advertisements floating in the air, towering skyscrapers with blinking lights, neon pink and cyan color palette, dense fog, Blade Runner aesthetic, cinematic photography style, ultra-detailed, atmospheric
```

## 适合主题

科幻场景、游戏封面、城市夜景、角色设计、科技品牌
