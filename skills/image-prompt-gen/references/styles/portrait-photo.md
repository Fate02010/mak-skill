# portrait-photo — 人像摄影

专业人像摄影风格，散景背景，自然真实感。

## 核心关键词

```
portrait photography, shallow depth of field, bokeh background, natural skin texture, soft catchlight in eyes
```

## 光照关键词

| 场景 | 关键词 |
|------|--------|
| 室外自然光 | `golden hour light, warm side lighting` |
| 室内窗光 | `soft window light, diffused natural light` |
| 专业棚拍 | `professional studio lighting, softbox, Rembrandt lighting` |
| 环境光 | `available light, ambient light` |

## 相机/镜头建议

- `shot on Canon EOS R5, 85mm f/1.4` — 经典人像
- `shot on Sony A7IV, 50mm f/1.8` — 自然写真
- `Leica M10, 35mm, film grain` — 胶片质感

## 负向提示词（SD/Flux）

```
deformed face, bad anatomy, blurry, low quality, watermark
```

## 完整提示词示例

```
a young Asian woman in her mid-20s, short bob haircut, wearing a soft cream linen blouse, standing in a sunlit park, golden hour light, warm side lighting, natural skin texture, shallow depth of field, bokeh background with green leaves, portrait photography, shot on Sony A7IV 85mm f/1.4, ultra-detailed
```

## 适合主题

人物肖像、写真、商业人像、证件照风格提升、情感故事
