# chinese-ink — 水墨画

中国传统水墨风格，大量留白，意境深远，禅意十足。

## 核心关键词

```
Chinese ink painting, sumi-e, ink wash, calligraphic brushstrokes, white space, zen aesthetic
```

## 风格变体

| 风格 | 关键词 |
|------|--------|
| 山水水墨 | `ink landscape, misty mountains, pine trees, Chinese scroll painting` |
| 花鸟水墨 | `ink painting of lotus, bamboo ink art, bird and flower painting` |
| 人物水墨 | `ink figure painting, flowing robes, expressive strokes` |
| 泼墨风格 | `splashed ink technique, spontaneous brushwork, abstract ink` |

## 技术特征

- `negative space as design element` — 留白即设计
- `varied ink tones from black to pale gray` — 墨色浓淡变化
- `xuan paper texture` — 宣纸纹理
- `dry brush and wet ink contrast` — 枯湿对比
- `calligraphic brush quality` — 书法笔意
- `minimal color, if any: light washes of blue, red, or green` — 淡彩点缀

## 完整提示词示例

```
a solitary mountain peak emerging from morning mist, scattered pine trees on rocky cliffs, a small pavilion barely visible below, traditional Chinese ink painting, sumi-e style, calligraphic brushstrokes, varied ink tones, extensive white space, xuan paper texture, contemplative and serene atmosphere
```

## 适合主题

山水、梅兰竹菊、荷花、松鹤、禅意场景
