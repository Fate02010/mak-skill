# new-chinese — 新中式

融合东方美学与现代设计语言，雅致而不沉闷，适合当代中国风。

## 核心关键词

```
new Chinese style, modern Chinese aesthetic, elegant, minimalist oriental design, contemporary Chinese art
```

## 风格特征

- `soft ink wash background` — 淡墨背景
- `minimalist composition with oriental elements` — 简约构图+东方元素
- `muted color palette: ivory, sage green, dusty rose, navy` — 低饱和色调
- `modern calligraphic typography` — 现代书法字体
- `traditional motifs in modern context` — 传统纹样的现代诠释
- `negative space balance` — 留白平衡

## 元素词库

云纹：`auspicious cloud patterns`
茶道：`tea ceremony, gaiwan, steam`
建筑：`Chinese garden, courtyard, moon gate`
纹样：`Chinese lattice pattern, blue-and-white porcelain`
服装：`hanfu, qipao, modern Chinese fashion`

a woman in a modern qipao-inspired outfit standing in a traditional Chinese garden with moon gate, new Chinese style, soft ink wash background, muted sage green and ivory palette, minimalist oriental composition, auspicious cloud motifs, contemporary Chinese aesthetic, elegant and refined atmosphere
