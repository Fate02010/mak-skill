# cinematic-photo — 电影感摄影

电影帧画面质感，色调分级，叙事性构图。

## 核心关键词

```
cinematic photography, film still, movie frame, anamorphic lens, cinematic color grading
```

## 色调类型

| 风格 | 关键词 |
|------|--------|
| 经典好莱坞 | `teal and orange color grading, Hollywood cinematic` |
| 文艺片 | `desaturated, muted tones, European arthouse cinema` |
| 惊悚/黑色 | `film noir, high contrast, deep shadows, black and white` |
| 暖色史诗 | `warm golden tones, epic cinematography` |
| 冷色科幻 | `cold blue tones, sci-fi atmosphere` |

## 必备元素

- `anamorphic lens flare` — 变形镜头光晕
- `shallow depth of field, bokeh` — 浅景深
- `widescreen 2.39:1` — 宽幅电影比例
- `grain, film texture` — 胶片颗粒感

## 完整提示词示例

```
a woman standing alone at the edge of a desert highway at sunset, teal and orange cinematic color grading, anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, dramatic volumetric light, golden dust particles in the air, film still, widescreen 2.39:1, cinematic photography, highly detailed, 8K
```

## 适合主题

人物故事、旅行、情绪表达、品牌故事片、MV 封面
